Africa is connected. Its money should be too.
Cross-border movement can still mean unnecessary conversions, unfamiliar payment methods, unclear rates and waiting for funds to arrive.

Wakala is built around a simpler idea: connect local markets and make movement easier to understand.

How Wakala works

Money movement without unnecessary complexity.

The journey from one local currency to another should feel like one continuous experience.

01 — FUND

Start with your local money.

Fund your Wakala wallet using supported payment methods available through your market and network.

02 — MOVE

Cross the network.

Choose a destination and review the applicable transfer details before you confirm.

03 — RECEIVE

Receive money ready to use.

The recipient receives funds in the supported local currency and can access available Wakala services.
