Important

Verification comes first.

Only Wakala administrators can verify submissions and approve payouts.

Reported impressions, likes, followers and other campaign requirements are subject to verification.

Repeated, artificial, purchased or otherwise ineligible engagement may be rejected. A submission does not guarantee payment until it has been reviewed and approved by an administrator.

Creator standard

Quality matters.

Wakala is looking for creators who can communicate clearly, entertain their audience and produce content people actually want to watch.

Followers and impressions alone do not guarantee approval. Content quality, originality, accuracy and campaign compliance are also considered.

Creator application

Create with Wakala.

Tell us about yourself and the type of content you want to create. Your application will be stored in the Wakala creator system and made available to administrators for review.

You can apply to different campaigns. Each campaign accepts one application per creator.

Your application is not a payout request.

Applications are reviewed separately from content submissions. If your application is accepted, you can create content and continue through the campaign verification process.
