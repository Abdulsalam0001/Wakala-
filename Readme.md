## 🌍 Why Wakala?

Africa is connected by people, businesses, families and opportunity.

But moving money across borders can still feel unnecessarily complicated.

Different countries have different currencies.

Different banks support different systems.

Different payment networks operate independently.

And services that should work together often don't.

**Wakala is being built to change that.**

We envision a platform where financial services feel less fragmented — where people can manage their money, move funds across supported corridors, access digital services and stay connected from one place.

---

# The Problem

Someone working in one African country may need to send money home.

A business may need to pay someone in another country.

A traveler may need connectivity when crossing a border.

A freelancer may receive money from another market.

A family may need to support someone thousands of kilometres away.

Yet these seemingly simple situations can involve:

* Multiple financial institutions
* Complicated transfer processes
* Currency differences
* Limited payment options
* High friction
* Slow settlement
* Poor visibility
* Disconnected digital services

The problem isn't that Africa lacks financial infrastructure.

**The problem is that much of it is fragmented.**

# 🌍 Designed for a Borderless Africa

Africa isn't one financial market.

Every country has its own:

* Currency
* Banks
* Payment networks
* Mobile-money ecosystems
* Financial institutions
* Regulations
* Infrastructure

Wakala is therefore designed to work **with the differences between markets**, rather than pretending they don't exist.

Supported corridors can be configured around the realities of each market.

That means the platform can grow from one corridor into many.
