The problem

Africa doesn't need more complexity.

Moving money between markets should feel as simple as sending money to someone next door. Wakala brings the pieces together around local currencies, familiar payment methods and a single wallet experience.

01

Too many payment rails

Different markets use different banks, wallets, mobile-money systems and card networks.

02

Currency friction

People often need to navigate multiple conversions before money reaches its destination.

03

Disconnected experiences

Wallets, payments and connectivity often live in separate products.

04

Borders create friction

Moving between markets should not mean learning an entirely new payment experience every time.
