The problem
Africa is connected.
Its money shouldn't feel disconnected.

Moving money across borders can still mean unnecessary conversions, unfamiliar payment methods, unclear rates and waiting for funds to arrive.

Wakala is being built around a simpler idea: connect local markets directly and make the movement of money easier to understand.

How Wakala works

Money movement without unnecessary complexity.

The goal is simple: make the journey from one local currency to another feel like one continuous experience.

01 — FUND

Start with your local money.

Fund your Wakala wallet using the supported payment methods available in your market.

02 — MOVE

Cross the network.

Choose the destination and see the applicable rate and transfer details before confirming.

03 — RECEIVE

Receive money ready to use.

The recipient receives funds in the supported local currency and can use the available Wakala services.
