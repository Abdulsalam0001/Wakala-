How Wakala works

Moving money should feel simple.

Wakala connects the right people, currencies and payment channels so that cross-border payments feel more like local payments.

01 / CHOOSE

Choose your destination

Select where your payment is going and the local currency you want your recipient to receive.

02 / CONNECT

Wakala connects the corridor

Our network connects your payment to the appropriate local partner, agent or payment channel.

03 / RECEIVE

Receive locally

Your recipient receives the payment through the available local Wakala channel.
